About

Built for
the boring part.

Most market content is designed to make you act. Buy this, watch that, don't miss out. Very little of it helps you understand what's actually happening or why it matters to a long-term plan.

This platform does two things. The weekly note explains what moved markets and why, in plain English — earnings, rates, inflation, and the reasoning behind the headlines. No calls, no positions, no urgency.

The journal handles the other half: a structured way to record your own decisions and the reasons behind them, so you can review your process over months instead of relying on memory. Outcomes are noisy. Reasoning is where the patterns live.

Read the note to understand the market. Use the journal to understand yourself. That's the whole offer — no signals, no tips, no picks.

The Approach

Process over
emotion.

Most people don't struggle because they picked the wrong stock. They struggle because position sizes were too large to survive being wrong, or because a plan written in calm conditions got abandoned in volatile ones.

Recording decisions changes that. Not the outcome of a trade — the reasoning behind it. Over months, written reasons reveal patterns that memory quietly edits out.

None of it is exciting. Checklists, logs, honest reviews. But it's the part you actually control, and it's the only part worth writing about.
