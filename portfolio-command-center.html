The Problem

Allocations drift quietly. Most people never notice.

A 60/40 becomes 72/28 without a single decision being made. No alert, no email, no moment where anything obviously goes wrong — just a portfolio that slowly stops being the one you designed.

You review your trades. When did you last review your portfolio?

The Portfolio Command Center makes drift visible — one dashboard tracking every holding against the targets you set yourself.

Questions

Frequently asked

What do I need to use it?

A free Google account, and that's it. The dashboard is built in Google Sheets — you'll get a link, click "Make a copy", and it saves to your own Drive. It works in any browser, on desktop, tablet and phone, and nothing is installed.

Does it update prices automatically?

The Google Sheets version can pull prices using built-in spreadsheet functions where available. You should always verify figures against your broker — treat the dashboard as an organisational tool, not a data source.

Does it tell me what to buy or sell?

No, and deliberately so. It shows you how far your portfolio has drifted from targets you set. Every decision remains entirely yours. This is not financial advice.

Do I need to be an experienced investor?

No. If you can enter what you own into a spreadsheet, you can use it. The structure does the rest.

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment of £11.99, the files are yours permanently.
